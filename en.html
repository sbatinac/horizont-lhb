SB
Owner & director

Founder · entrepreneur · technology & creativity

One vision. Many paths. No limits.

Behind every business success there is an idea. Behind every idea there is a person who dared to try.

I have never believed that a person should be defined by a single job, a single activity or a single career path.

To me, entrepreneurship is much more than doing business. It is a constant search for possibilities, the ability to spot opportunities and the courage to step into something new.

My business experience covers sales, trade, building business relationships, negotiation and connecting partners across different markets. At the same time, my passion for technology and creativity takes me through computer systems, web design, digital production, artificial intelligence, and video and audio editing.

Seemingly different worlds. To me, parts of the same story.
A story of creating, connecting and constantly moving forward.

I don’t claim to know everything. But I know how to learn, explore, find a solution and bring the right people together around a good idea.

When a problem comes up, I don’t look for a reason to give up. I look for a way to solve it.

When I spot a business opportunity, I don’t just watch it from afar. I try to turn it into a real project.

And when I start something new, I approach it with the same seriousness, responsibility and drive to make the result as good as it can be.

Over the years I have learned that success doesn’t always take the shortest road. Sometimes it takes a change of direction, new beginnings, hard decisions and the willingness to start again.

But that is exactly where I find my strength.

I don’t want to be limited by what I do today. I want to discover everything I can achieve tomorrow.

I believe in knowledge, professionalism, creativity and fair business. I believe real opportunity is born when experience meets an idea, and the idea meets determination.

That is why I don’t build my future around one line of business.
I build it around possibilities.

Because my biggest project has never been just one product, one service or one company.

My biggest project is creating a future in which ideas become reality.

Many activities. One vision. Unlimited ambition.
